Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a quiz?

Select your grade, pick a math topic, choose a difficulty level (Easy, Medium, or Hard), and tap "Start Quiz!" You'll get 10 timed questions.

What grades are supported?

Grades 1 through 8, aligned with the CBSE curriculum. Each grade unlocks age-appropriate topics.

How does scoring work?

You earn stars based on accuracy: 90%+ = 3 stars, 70-89% = 2 stars, 50-69% = 1 star. Your scores are saved in the History screen.

Can I turn off sounds?

Yes. Tap the gear icon on the home screen to open Settings, where you can toggle sound effects and haptic feedback.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Math With Matthew works entirely offline. No internet connection is needed.

How do I clear my quiz history?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and tap "Clear All History."
