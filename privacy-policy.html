Math With Matthew Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 23, 2026

Math With Matthew ("the App") is a math quiz game for children. We take your family's privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Data We Do NOT Collect

Math With Matthew does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No photos, camera, or microphone access
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No cookies or web tracking
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

Data Stored On Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Quiz scores and history — your results from completed quizzes
  • App settings — your sound and haptic feedback preferences

This data never leaves your device. It is stored using iOS UserDefaults and can be deleted at any time by clearing history in the App's Settings screen, or by deleting the App.

Children's Privacy

Math With Matthew is designed for children ages 6 and older. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. Since we do not collect any data from any user, including children, no parental consent is required.

Third-Party Services

Math With Matthew does not use any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Internet Access

The current version of Math With Matthew works entirely offline. Future versions may optionally download additional question packs from our servers. If implemented, this will only download question content and will not transmit any personal data.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated through an App update.
